Safe Recycling Instructions

  1. Back up important files and erase personal data from phones, computers and storage devices.
  2. Remove batteries only when they are designed to be removed safely. Do not handle damaged or swollen batteries.
  3. Never burn, break or dismantle electronic devices at home.
  4. Keep electronic waste separate from regular household rubbish.
  5. Take devices to an authorized collection or recycling centre and follow its instructions.
  6. Keep the collection receipt or reference number to help track processing status.
