Recycling Instructions

  1. Back up your files and remove personal data from devices.
  2. Keep batteries separate where safe; never place damaged batteries in regular bins.
  3. Do not dismantle, burn, or break electronic equipment.
  4. Deliver items to an authorized collection or recycling centre.
  5. Keep your collection receipt to track processing status.
